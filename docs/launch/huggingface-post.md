Debussy 0.1 is out: an open **System 1 decision model** for AI agents 🌙

One pass per decision, a probability for every option:
- 🛡️ safety: harmful request / refusal / harmful reply
- 🔧 tool calls: call the tool, ask for details, or say it can't help
- 🤔 knowledge, with an honest "I don't know"

Built on Qwen3.5-4B and K2-Horizon-7B (thinking switched off), calibrated with tiny heads, developed on a 4-core CPU.
Estimated Decision Index ≈ 63.7 (our estimate, not official). We also published what didn't work.
