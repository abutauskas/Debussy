# Code of conduct

Debussy Labs wants this project to be a place where anyone can contribute.

- **Be respectful.** Critique ideas and code, never people. No harassment, insults or discrimination of any kind.
- **Be honest.** Report results as you measured them, credit other people's work, and say when you're unsure.
- **Be helpful.** Assume good faith, explain your reasoning, and welcome newcomers.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributions that break these rules, and may block people who keep
breaking them. To report a problem privately, contact the maintainers through the email on https://debussylabs.com or
GitHub's private reporting (see `SECURITY.md`).
